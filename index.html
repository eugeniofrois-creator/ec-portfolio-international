Before we begin

Do you offer maintenance after delivery?

Yes, for websites I develop. Maintenance is contracted separately, with agreed updates, fixes, fees, response times and support period. Larger changes are quoted separately.

Can we work remotely?

Yes. Project requirements, feedback and delivery can be coordinated in writing. I’m based in Brazil, in the Brasília time zone.

Can I pay through PayPal?

For direct projects, PayPal payment arrangements can be discussed after the quote. Currency, fees and milestones must be agreed first. This website does not process payments. Projects found through freelance platforms must follow their payment rules.

Will I receive the source files?

Source files are included in the agreed delivery. Ownership, third-party asset licences and any reuse restrictions are specified in the project agreement.

Do you build complex applications?

My current freelance focus is small static websites and interface improvements. Complex backend systems, sensitive data processing and payment integrations require a separate technical assessment.
